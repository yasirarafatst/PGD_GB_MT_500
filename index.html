What this simulator is based on

  • PGD-GB's publicly stated objectives emphasize aptitude, knowledge, know-how/skills, attitude, management knowledge and garment-industry trade knowledge.
  • The question bank therefore emphasizes English, quantitative aptitude, analytical reasoning, management/business and RMG/garment-business knowledge.
  • Questions marked PYQ-pattern are practice questions modeled on recurring admission-test skill areas; they are not reproduced official past papers.
  • Questions marked Expected are forward-looking practice questions based on PGD-GB objectives and contemporary garment-business themes.

Official references checked: IBA PGD-GB program information and publicly available 2024/2025 PGD-GB admission-result/interview documents.
